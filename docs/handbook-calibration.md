# Bounded handbook rewriting

Apply only to a confirmed maintained source or an explicit provisional maintenance assignment.
Keep original attribution. Third-party official distributions stay read-only; installation, local
write access and a fork are not evidence of ownership.

One round produces three concrete artifacts:

1. **Prewritten manual**: read the current entry and execution-critical references, templates and
   scripts. Rewrite for the current executor. Preserve non-inferable facts, contracts, authority,
   private-data boundaries and demonstrated pitfalls. Remove redundant explanations or move genuinely
   conditional detail to a referenced guide; do not move an entire old body merely to claim a smaller
   entry. Record the baseline, changed/deferred items and required invariants. An already minimal
   entry can yield identical bytes with an explicit reason.
2. **Independent execution**: a different agent receives the candidate, a realistic bounded request
   and raw inputs, without the author's expected answer or suspected fix. Produce actual artifacts
   or command results. Distinguish offline/synthetic acceptance from live service/device behavior.
   For a dangerous or unavailable workflow, execute its authorized bounded component and name the gap.
3. **Post-execution revision**: fix the concrete issues found, or retain the candidate when no issue
   was observed. Re-run only an affected check after a material change. Default to one rewrite,
   one execution and one revision; unresolved problems get an explicit disposition instead of an
   open-ended optimization loop.

Completion means critical contracts survived, dependencies resolve, the chosen task produced its
requested result, and observed problems were fixed or explicitly held. It does not require proving
an abstract maximum benefit, globally shortest manual, or universal model quality improvement.
Record the observed model identifier when available; otherwise use `unknown`, not a guessed version.
Keep one canonical manual by default. Create model-specific adapters only for an observed stable
behavioral difference, not automatically for every model release.

## Accumulate issues; do not rewrite on every use

A usage record identifies Skill/source revision, observed model (or unknown), distinct task,
problem category and code, local evidence reference, and disposition. Keep prompts, credentials and
private task contents out of repository records; local artifacts may remain under the user's control.
The [experimental pilot](handbook-pilot.md) implements a bounded version of these observations.

- Invalid commands/interfaces, incorrect facts and missing critical boundaries trigger a focused
  correction review as soon as observed.
- The same ambiguity, missing operational fact or unnecessary step in two independent tasks triggers
  a local rewrite review. Several events or turns of one task count once.
- A meaningful executor-model or tool-environment change triggers representative recalibration,
  not automatic corpus-wide rewriting.
- A one-off task preference or unexplained failure is recorded without becoming a universal rule.
- A periodic review inspects accumulated issues. No new evidence means no required rewrite.

Triggers authorize a review within the operator's standing scope, not blind file mutation, capture
activation or third-party patches. Implement reviewed corrections in their canonical repository,
validate, merge, then activate exact published bytes with backups and current-hash guards.

## Pilot before a standard practice

Observe two or three independent real tasks after activation. Preserve synthetic validation in a
separate evidence set. Check whether the observed manual was actually used, the requested work was
completed, essential constraints held, concrete issues were actionable, and recording caused no
material disruption. Missing outcome or hook correlation stays unknown.

At two reports, review the evidence; a third can clarify an unresolved observation. The pilot stops
admitting new sessions at its configured limit. Do not enlarge the sample simply to manufacture a
positive result. A small pilot can justify a reviewed integration proposal with explicit limits;
it cannot establish universal effectiveness. Keep the feature experimental until real evidence
supports adoption through the normal source-review process. No automatic promotion or self-rewrite.
